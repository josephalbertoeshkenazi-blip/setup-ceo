Export all of my stored memories and everything you have learned about me from past conversations. Use my own words whenever you can, especially for instructions and preferences.

## Categories (in this order):

1. **Instructions**: rules I asked you to follow: tone, format, style, "always do X", "never do Y", and corrections to how you behave. Only from your stored memories, not from loose conversations.

2. **Identity**: name, city, studies, languages and personal interests.

3. **Work**: current and past roles, companies and areas I know how to work in.

4. **Projects**: projects I built or genuinely committed to. One per line: what it does, where it stands and important decisions. Start the line with the project name.

5. **Preferences**: opinions, tastes and ways of working that apply in general.

## Format:

One heading per category. Inside each, one line per item, from the oldest date to the newest:

[YYYY-MM-DD] - The item.

If you do not know the date, use [no date].

## Output:
- Put everything inside a single code block so it is easy to copy.
- After the block, say whether that is everything or whether there is more.
